TITLE: Pixel Timeline — fifty-two years of pixels, dragged by hand
PROMPT: A horizontal, arcade-neon museum corridor of the pixel itself: Pong to neural upscaling, each milestone a glowing plaque with a procedurally generated pixel-art sigil, and a scrubber rail that behaves like a hi-hat of nostalgia.
DESCRIPTION: A midnight-blue scrolling lane where twelve events sit alternately top and bottom along a 1970–2026 scale: year in neon teal, headline, one-line epitaph in dim lilac. Each plaque gets a 16×16 sprite rendered live from a seeded PRNG and mirrored on its axis — half pixel-art angel, half birthday-cake candle — displayed crisp at 32px via image-rendering: pixelated. The overhead rail holds decade ticks and a pink scrub-head with neon bloom that tracks scroll position; the whole corridor is grab-draggable (mouse and touch), wheel-scrolls vertically into horizontally, and answers arrow keys.
TECHNIQUES: seeded symmetric micro-sprite generation on tiny canvases scaled without smoothing, oversized absolutely-positioned timeline lane with pointer-drag panning, wheel-axis remapping with non-passive preventDefault, scroll-to-rail-head percentage binding, alternating card placement from index parity.
INTERACTION: DRAG the corridor, spin the MOUSE WHEEL, or lean on the arrow keys; hover any plaque for a brighter border, and ride the pink head down the rail from Pong to the models dreaming new pixels.
