TITLE: Alexandria — the index that outlived the fire
PROMPT: Map the whole impossible Library as a treemap: seven wings sized by true scroll-count, each full of named holdings, so you can see that Aristotle’s lost dialogues outweigh the entire religion wing — then click your way into the stacks with a breadcrumb trail.
DESCRIPTION: A slice-and-dice treemap of an imagined complete catalogue: Astronomy, Poetry, Medicine, Engineering, History, Philosophy and Religion partition the papyrus plate by weight, children re-partition inside each umber wing in alternating directions, parchment-tinted leaves labelled when there’s room and title-tipped always. Clicking a wing zooms the view to its internal layout; the dotted breadcrumb strip lets you hop between any depth of the archive, and the header keeps the pre-fire total honest.
TECHNIQUES: weight-proportional slice-and-dice layout with alternating split axes and sorted children, click-through drill navigation on a path stack with breadcrumb reconstruction, palette-keyed depth styling (umber wings, parchment leaves), label-visibility gating by cell geometry, DOM-cell treemap with title tooltips.
INTERACTION: Click any umber wing to dissolve it into its holdings, click the breadcrumbs (or the Great Library itself) to climb back out, hover any leaf for its exact scroll count, and watch how badly Philosophy hoards the real estate.
