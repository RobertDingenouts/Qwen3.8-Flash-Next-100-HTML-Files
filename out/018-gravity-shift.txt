TITLE: Gravity Shift — tetrominoes with a flip switch
PROMPT: Take the falling-blocks ritual and give it one heretical button: gravity is a suggestion, and the player's real skill is stacking towers that survive being dropped upward.
DESCRIPTION: A compact pastel tetromino arcade in a lavender-night shell. The INVERT button (or space bar) flips which ceiling the pieces fall toward — stacks build from both ends and lines clear in either regime. Rotation carries wall-kick nudges; speed accelerates with every line; a fat arrow beside the board flips to announce which way is down. Swipe up on the board to invert on touch, swipe sideways to slide, tap to rotate.
TECHNIQUES: offset-array tetromino model with matrix rotation and kick-based wall testing, direction-agnostic gravity ticking and locking, splice/unshift line clearing with bottom-up scan, bevelled pseudo-3D block rendering, DPR letterboxed playfield sizing, touch-gesture input layer, keyboard-first arcade wiring.
INTERACTION: ← → move, ↑ (or ROTATE button/tap) spins, ↓ soft-drops, SPACE or the pink INVERT button reverses gravity — stack four lines before the ceiling fights back, NEW GAME to restart after a collapse.
