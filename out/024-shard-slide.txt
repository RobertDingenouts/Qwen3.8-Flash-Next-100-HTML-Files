TITLE: Shard Slide — a sliding puzzle of paintings that never hang straight
PROMPT: Treat the fifteen-puzzle as a museum crisis: a generative abstraction has been sawn into sixteen shards and misaligned in its gilded frame, and only a patient registrar can slide it back into coherence — then re-hang a brand-new painting from the vault.
DESCRIPTION: A gallery wall vignette with a gilt frame and wall placard around a 4×4 sliding puzzle whose tiles are slices of a procedurally painted abstract — arcs, bezier gestural strokes and pigment speckle in one of five curated palettes. Tiles slide with eased offsets (the paint itself moves, the slices never re-sort), the placard counts moves, and solving the board makes the frame glow and the label read RESTORED.
TECHNIQUES: seeded generative abstract painting rendered once to an offscreen canvas, slice-and-draw tile rendering from source rectangles, offset-tween slide animation keyed to move commits, legal-move-only keyboard shuffle guaranteeing solvability, arrow-key + click tile-into-blank input, museum furniture typography and gilt-frame CSS.
INTERACTION: Click any shard beside the gap (or press arrow keys) to slide it, retrace or count your way to the restored composition, RE-SCRAMBLE re-hangs the same work crooked, RE-HANG pulls a fresh painting from the vault.
