TITLE: Mood Lamp — ambient colour-noise with five personalities
PROMPT: A full-screen lava lamp for people who don’t want a lava lamp: slow radial colour fields that drift, breathe and bleed into each other, with five named moods and no chrome except a row of pill buttons floating at the bottom.
DESCRIPTION: Five giant hsla radial-gradient blobs wander the viewport at drift velocities with per-blob sine wobble, wrapping off-screen; each breathes its radius on its own phase, and a translucent dark veil every frame makes their overlaps smear into plasma-like blends instead of clean circles. Each preset (aurora, ember, deep sea, orchard, static rose) re-seeds all five blobs with a hue triad, a global speed, and a one-line epitaph under the masthead. Selecting a new mood wipes the plate to black so the colours arrive honestly rather than haunting the old palette.
TECHNIQUES: drifting radial-gradient field painting with alpha-persistence smearing, per-blob phase-offset breathing and wrap-around kinematics, hue-triad preset architecture with reseeded state, backdrop-blur pill UI floating over canvas, DPR viewport sizing.
INTERACTION: Tap a mood pill (aurora defaults, static rose is the best thinking colour), then look away slowly — the lamp is designed to be peripheral, and it will still be breathing when you come back.
