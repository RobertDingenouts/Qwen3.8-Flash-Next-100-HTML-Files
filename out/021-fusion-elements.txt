TITLE: Fusion Lattice — 2048 on the periodic table

INTERACTION: Swipe or arrow-slide the lattice to fuse elements, chase the sodium at 2048 (play keeps going past it), watch ENERGY climb and BEST remember your run, RE-IGNITE restarts the accelerator.
