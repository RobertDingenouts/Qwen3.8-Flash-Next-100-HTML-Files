TITLE: Megacity Race — six cities that do not exist
PROMPT: Run the addictive bar-chart race on a future nobody has scheduled yet: 2000 to 2077, six invented megacities, seeded sigmoid growth curves, scripted disasters, and a serif ticker that drops each year’s canonical heartbreak.
DESCRIPTION: A warm poster infographic with one enormous year in the corner and six fat pastel bars ranked from bottom (small) to top (huge), populations rendered in millions with monospace value tags riding the bars. Growth follows per-city logistic curves around hidden pivot years, multiplied by a late-century boom term and seeded jitter; scripted events actually bend the data — Uralgrad’s fusion exodus bleeds its bar until a 2070 reboot, Batavia Prime loses a slice after the Sunda flood, Caspia Nova is born from a dam in 2050 and rockets. Rows slide past each other with springy transforms while the italic ticker narrates the milestones.
TECHNIQUES: parametric logistic population synthesis with seeded PRNG texture, scripted multiplicative event curves wired to a serif event ticker, rank-based row reordering with CSS transform transitions, year scrubber decoupled from playback rate, value-label tracking on animated widths.
INTERACTION: WATCH it run, PAUSE at any year to read the standings, SCRUB the timeline anywhere in 2000–2077 to fast-forward futures or rewind the exoduses, and ↺ 2000 to run the century again.
