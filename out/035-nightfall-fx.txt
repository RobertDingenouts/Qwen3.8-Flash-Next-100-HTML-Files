TITLE: Nightfall FX — a pyrotechnic notebook
PROMPT: Give the visitor a safe night sky and a full powder notebook: six real firework shell types with distinct physics personalities, and every tap must feel like writing a small bright sentence the whole horizon can read.
DESCRIPTION: A midnight harbour scene — fading sky washes, twinkling stars, a jagged town silhouette. Clicks launch rockets from the waterfront that arc and burst into peonies, drooping gold willows, tilted rings, fan palms, two-tone doubles and crackling chrysanthemums; each type has its own particle count, drag, gravity and twinkle. Additive 'lighter' compositing with a translucent fade builds real phosphor bloom. A CURTAIN mode gives a perpetual show and FINALE fires a fourteen-shell volley.
TECHNIQUES: rocket ballistics with burst-at-apex detection, six particle emission profiles (uniform sphere, ring, fan, layered palms), additive compositing + alpha-decay persistence for glow trails, per-particle twinkle phase, procedural skyline via deterministic hash sines, star field phase animation.
INTERACTION: Click or tap anywhere in the sky to throw a shell toward that exact point, CURTAIN to keep a slow rain of shells going, FINALE! for a rapid volley, DARK SKY to snuff every ember and start over.
