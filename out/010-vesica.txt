TITLE: Vesica Piscis — a compass construction told in seven breaths
PROMPT: Turn sacred geometry into a liturgy: the page should draw the Flower of Life exactly as a mason would — step by step, compass pen visibly walking each arc — while an italic narrator explains each act, and the visitor simply presses “later” to keep the ceremony going.
DESCRIPTION: A parchment plate inside a matted frame, an SVG construction canvas, and seven narrated stages: the point, the first circle, the second circle that births the vesica, the crossed axes, the six petals of the Seed of Life, the ring of twelve, and the great containing circle. Each arc is drawn by a red pen-dot travelling the path while the stroke reveals behind it.
TECHNIQUES: hand-built SVG arc paths for compass circles, stroke-dasharray/dashoffset reveal animation, animated marker via getTotalLength/getPointAtLength, stage machine with sequential chaining and instant rewind by opacity reset, click-through dot navigation, keyboard arrows.
INTERACTION: Press “later →” / “← earlier” or arrow keys to advance or rewind one ceremony stage, or click any of the seven rubric dots to jump; arcs animate as they are “drawn”, the caption voice follows along.
