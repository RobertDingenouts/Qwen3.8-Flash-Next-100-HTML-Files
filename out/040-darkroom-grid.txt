TITLE: Darkroom Grid — develop the print by going dark
PROMPT: Recast Lights Out as a darkroom ritual: the puzzle board is a photographic print cut into tiles, every tile still lit by the safelight is underexposed red glass — quench the whole grid and the sunset finishes developing beneath your hands.
DESCRIPTION: A procedurally painted dusk photograph (gradient sky, glowing sun, twin mountain ridges, lake reflection stripes, shoreline silhouette and film grain) is sliced into an n×n mosaic. Lit tiles wear a pulsing dark-red safelight glaze; taps toggle the classic plus-shaped pattern. Puzzles are generated by scrambling from the solved state, so every negative is guaranteed solvable. Win with every tile dark and the print breathes warm light with a title dissolve; move counter tracks your restraint.
TECHNIQUES: procedural landscape painting on an offscreen canvas (gradients, ridges via sine sums, reflection stripes, grain pass), tile-mosaic drawImage cropping with mortar gaps, lights-out parity game with guaranteed-solvable generation, pulsing overlay animation keyed to Date.now, pointer-to-cell hit testing that respects grid gaps.
INTERACTION: Tap a tile to flood it and its four neighbours with safelight, pick the grid size (4×4, 5×5, 6×6), FRESH NEGATIVE cuts a new scramble, and leave every tile dark to finish the print.
