TITLE: Hex Atlas of Nowhere Specific — cartography by committee of one
PROMPT: A procedural fantasy map in iron-gall ink on parchment: value-noise continents with a sensible coastline falloff, six ink-washed biomes, mountain chevrons, a compass rose — and a name generator that lets the visitor colonise every hex.
DESCRIPTION: Fifteen by eleven pointy-ish hexes fill a canvas plate: elevation is three-octave smoothed value noise modulated by a quadratic island falloff, so realms always arrive with coasts; six band colours read as a surveyor’s wash — deep shelf blue, sand, meadow, forest, highland, snow-cap. Stroked hex seams keep the cartographic register; high-elevation cells sprout tiny ink triangles; a hand-drawn compass rose with a red north spike guards the lower corner. Click any hex and the syllable engine christens it — coastal cells get bay and abyss vocabulary — and labels it in italic until you click again to revoke the treaty. NEW REALM rolls an entirely new world with its seed shown in base-36 for descendants.
TECHNIQUES: multi-octave value noise with smoothstep interpolation and island gradient, biome classification by elevation bands, odd-column offset hex layout with hit-test inverse mapping, seeded syllable-pair name generation with terrain-aware suffix banks, canvas compass decoration drawing.
INTERACTION: Click hexes to found settlements (click again to un-found them), NEW REALM ▸ for a fresh planet, and read your realm’s seed in the corner — generation is reproducible if your grandchildren need the map back.
