TITLE: Grid Reveal — the numbers know the picture first
PROMPT: Build a Picross parlour on indigo: eight hand-authored 10×10 pixel pictures, honest row/column run-clues, and two brushes — one that gilds squares and one that dots the ones the numbers have ruled out.
DESCRIPTION: A violet-dark nonogram desk where gold row clues hug the left and column clues stand along the bottom, with bold seam lines every fifth row and column. Left-drag paints gilded squares, right-drag (or the MARK ✕ toggle for touch users) dots the dead cells, and a live ✕ counter reports how many painted squares disagree with the truth. A GLIMPSE fills one random correct cell when you're stuck; winning means every revealed square matches the clues exactly.
TECHNIQUES: run-length clue generation for rows and columns, dual-brush paint/dot drag interaction with pointer-button detection and a touch-mode toggle, live miscount scoring, exact-win comparison against the hidden picture, adaptive clue-label sizing with grid cell size, responsive canvas with block separators.
INTERACTION: Paint ■ with left-drag, dot ✕ with right-drag or MARK ✕ mode, watch the ✕ counter warn you, GLIMPSE for one honest free square, CLEAR the board or NEXT PICTURE for the next of eight hidden drawings.
