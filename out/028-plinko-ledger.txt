TITLE: Plinko Ledger — the house always rounds in its favour
PROMPT: Build the coin-drop gambling toy as a dry accounting gag: a green audit table, a gold peg field, and a bottom row of multipliers the board "pays out" — while a running payout ratio quietly proves the house is solvent.
DESCRIPTION: A nine-row staggered peg pyramid on dark felt with a glowing rail; click anywhere on the top rail to release a tumbling gold coin with a fading comet trail. Coins scatter off pegs with restitution plus a pinch of chaos, land in thirteen weighted bins, and every hit both bumps the bin's gold plate and stacks a bar in a live histogram. Drop counters and a running payout-multiple read out the audit in progress.
TECHNIQUES: circle-circle peg collisions with normal reflection, restitution and jitter, staggered lattice geometry from arithmetic rows, histogram with bump-flash animation per bin, coin radial-gradient metal shading, trail rendering from position queues, responsive felt-card layout with serif ledger typography.
INTERACTION: Tap the rail to place a coin mid-air, DROP A COIN to risk one from centre, RELEASE 24 for an avalanche to see the law of large numbers bite, CLOSE THE BOOKS wipes the audit clean.
