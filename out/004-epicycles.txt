TITLE: Epicycle Machine — Fourier dissection of a gesture
PROMPT: Make the discrete Fourier transform physical: the visitor draws a single freehand stroke, the page decomposes it into rotating phasors, and a necklace of spinning circles re-draws the drawing perfectly, with the visitor controlling how many harmonics the machine is allowed to keep.
DESCRIPTION: A graph-paper workbench where a sketched line is resampled to 256 points, run through a hand-written DFT, and replayed as a chain of up to 120 epicycles sorted by amplitude — big circles do the sweeping moves, small ones recover the wobble. The sketch layer and the spinning-phasor layer are separate canvases so the ink stays pristine while the machine turns. A built-in rose curve stands in for hands that prefer not to sketch.
TECHNIQUES: hand-coded DFT with negative-frequency folding, arc-length path resampling, centroid/scale normalisation, per-frame polyline extension on a persistent ink canvas, overlay epicycle canvas, live harmonic-count and speed sliders, CSS graph-paper background.
INTERACTION: Sketch a stroke with finger or mouse, slide HARMONICS to coarsen or sharpen the reconstruction, SPEED to slow the phasors, DEMO CURVE for a built-in figure-eight rose, CLEAR to wipe the bench.
