TITLE: Untangle Stars — pull the constellation apart
PROMPT: Take Planarity, move it to the observatory: a guaranteed-untieable knot of glowing stars and silk threads where every thread that crosses another hisses red, and the whole chart is silent only when the graph lies flat.
DESCRIPTION: Each level generates a provably planar random graph — a random spanning tree plus extra edges accepted only if they don't cross in a hidden "true" embedding — then scatters the stars onto a ring to tangle it. Drag any star freely; crossings are recomputed live and both threads involved glow angry red with pulsing intensity; star halos take on the same warning tint when a hot thread touches them. Clearing all crossings earns the NEXT KNOT button a denser constellation (7→14 stars).
TECHNIQUES: planar graph generation via union-find spanning tree + non-crossing edge admission against a secret embedding, orientation-based segment intersection tests for live crossing detection, per-edge bad-flagging with shared state, radial-gradient star glows with breathing radii, drag-anywhere node editing.
INTERACTION: Drag stars to move them (touch or mouse), watch the ✕ counter climb or fall, untie the knot to zero crossings, then NEW KNOT (or after a win) tightens the puzzle with one more star each time.
