TITLE: Dot Munch — one dotmuncher, one very patient ghost
PROMPT: Distill the arcade's first great duel to essentials: one maze, one snackeer, one ghost with perfect pathfinding — and let the four big dots flip the food chain for eight glowing seconds.
DESCRIPTION: A hand-drawn 13×11 maze in midnight blue with power pellets pulsing. The player interpolates cell-to-cell with buffered turns and a chomping wedge mouth; the ghost BFS-pathfinders to the player's cell every step (and away when frightened, eyes-white panic included, with googly eyes that track the prey). Fright mode tints the maze, drains a timer bar, and eating the ghost relocates it home.
TECHNIQUES: BFS distance fields for enemy pathfinding and flee mode, grid movement with sub-cell interpolation and turn buffering, mouth-arc rendering from velocity angle, power pellet state machine with blink-out timer, lives/score persistence across quick resets, responsive square-scaled canvas sizing.
INTERACTION: Arrows / WASD / swipes to munch (turns buffer at walls), chomp every pellet to clear the maze, grab a big dot to hunt the ghost for bonus points, get caught and you lose a life — tap or R to restart when the ghost even's the score.
