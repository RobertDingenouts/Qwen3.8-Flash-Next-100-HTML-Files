TITLE: Star Namer — connect the dots before the astronomers do
PROMPT: A celestial charting toy: a fresh procedural sky of twinkling magnitude-varied stars, click-through constellation stringing in dotted gold, and a name-field that turns your kitchen-table asterism into a permanent claim on localStorage.
DESCRIPTION: A navy observatory wall where stars are seeded points of varied magnitude — bright ones haloed in gold, all gently twinkling on individual sine phases — over a soft radial sky gradient. Clicking a star threads a dotted gold line from the previous pick, ringed nodes marking the claimed chain. UNDO snips the last edge, WIPE SKY starts a new figure, NEW SKY reseeds a completely different heaven. CLAIM writes your typed name into the archive: it joins the saved ticker, persists between visits, and the most recent claim ghost-writes itself in enormous italic across the sky, because empires.
TECHNIQUES: seeded procedural starfield with per-star twinkle phase, click-chain edge model with self-edge tolerance and undo stack, node-ring highlighting from an adjacency set, localStorage persistence of named constellations with graceful parse fallback, DPR canvas over fluid layout height.
INTERACTION: Click stars in order to draw your figure (first click starts the string), NAME IT and press ✓ CLAIM to archive it forever, UNDO for astronomer’s remorse, and NEW SKY when this heaven no longer flatters you.
