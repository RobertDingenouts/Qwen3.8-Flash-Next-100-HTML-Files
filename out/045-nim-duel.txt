TITLE: Nim Duel — misère matches against the machine
PROMPT: A quiet green baize duel of pure strategy: four rows of lit matches, misère rules, and an opponent who computes the nim-sum the way a metronome keeps time — until the endgame parity trap where you can still out-think it.
DESCRIPTION: The classic 1-3-5-7 match tableau painted on felt with a warm lamp vignette. Clicking any match burns it and everything to its right in its row: the matches tip, char, and go out with a fading flame halo. The machine answers after a theatrical pause, played with correct nim-sum strategy (making the xor zero) until only single-match rows remain, where it switches to misère parity play. Whichever side is forced to take the final match loses, and the turn banner keeps score in capitals.
TECHNIQUES: nim-sum strategy engine with misère endgame switch, click-to-take suffix semantics with per-match hover rows, burning match animation (rotation, char gradient, radial flame glow), row/column pointer hit testing on canvas, game-over state broadcast to DOM banner.
INTERACTION: Click any match to burn it plus its right-side neighbours, force the machine into taking the last match, NEW DUEL restarts the 1-3-5-7 tableau — and yes, the machine plays perfectly.
