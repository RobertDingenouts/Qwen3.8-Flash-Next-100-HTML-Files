TITLE: Prism Break — brickout with a paddle that is a prism
PROMPT: Re-imagine brick-breaker as an optics toy: the ball is a ray of white light, the paddle is a prism bar, and every return splits the ray into red, green and blue beams that shatter rainbow bricks — a breakout you can lose, win, and lose colour in.
DESCRIPTION: A neon-dark arcade field of spectrum-gradient bricks. One white ray serves from a tri-colour prism paddle; hitting the paddle diffracts it into three tinted rays (up to five live at once). Rays punch glowing particle bursts out of bricks, adopt brick colours, and vanish off the bottom — lose every ray and a life; clear every brick and the next band (a denser spectrum) rolls in.
TECHNIQUES: multi-ball arcade physics with paddle-offset reflection angles, diffraction (ball split on prism hit), hue-laid brick gradient with fade-out death animation, radial-gradient glow sprites, particle system, pointer-tracking paddle with keyboard-free touch play, live HUD in Century Gothic/monospace.
INTERACTION: Slide the paddle with mouse or finger, click/tap to serve or relight after a loss — time your returns off the prism edges to fan the three beams wide and clear the band.
