TITLE: Specimen Cabinet — the font you never chose, exhibited
PROMPT: A type specimen poster for the system fonts everyone uses and nobody thanks: a giant weight-lettered AaBb header, seven descending sizes that all obey one dragged axis, and a tracking slider that can make Verdana weep or sing.
DESCRIPTION: A strict black-and-white gallery wall: oversized AaBb set in the live family/weight/tracking state at fluid 13vw, above a ladder of pangram rows at scaled fractions of the SIZE slider — each row labelled with its pixel size in a monospace margin note. The TRACKING dial moves letterspacing across everything at once; the WEIGHT select hits 300–900 where the family offers it; six stack options swap the entire exhibit between grotesque, serif, monospace, humanist, Times and Verdana; three pangrams rotate (a fox variant, an anagram line, and a glyph/numeral parade). A status footer reports the exact live combo.
TECHNIQUES: single-axis size scaling with fractional row ladders, live font-stack swapping across the whole document tree, em-based tracking bound to a range, weight/stack/pangram state bound to selects and re-rendered in one pass, fluid display type via clamp.
INTERACTION: DRAG SIZE to walk the whole family portrait up and down, push TRACKING until the words fall apart, flip WEIGHT, cycle STACKS to watch Georgia and ui-monospace argue, and change the PANGRAM when you’re done blaming the alphabet.
