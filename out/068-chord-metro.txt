TITLE: Chord Metro — the drunk cartographer’s interchange diagram
PROMPT: Express a metro network as a chord diagram and nothing else: twelve stations on a chalk ring, six lines drawn as arcs through the middle, where the whole topology, every interchange, reads at a glance or collapses to a single glowing passenger’s route at a click.
DESCRIPTION: Twelve named stops (Alder Gate to Lantern Fields) ring a chalk plate; six transit lines are quadratic chords bowed toward the centre, each in its own route colour with round caps and 75% chalk-opacity. Node size grows with the number of live lines serving a stop, so interchanges bloat into majors. Clicking a station traces only its lines at full weight and fades every other arc to whisper-alpha, with the info line naming the lines in prose; the legend toggles whole lines out of existence, shrinking affected stations as the network simplifies.
TECHNIQUES: chord-diagram layout on a polar station ring, quadratic curves centripetally bowed for readable crossings, focus-mode alpha choreography across three visual classes, station-degree sizing, line visibility state with legend binding, responsive circular canvas.
INTERACTION: Click a station to see exactly how the city reaches it, click empty chalk to un-focus, and strike legend routes on/off to rehearse what the network looks like when the Harbour Line is on strike again.
