TITLE: Powder Room — four elements, one cellular automaton
PROMPT: A falling-sand sandbox distilled to essentials: ochre sand that piles and slumps, blue water that floods sideways, immovable stone, and an eraser — all on a glass UI panel in a dim room, counted honestly at the bottom.
DESCRIPTION: A 3-pixel-cell grid stepped bottom-up with alternating scan directions so nothing cheats left: each grain of sand falls, then tries its two lower diagonals at random order; each drop of water does the same plus pure lateral spread, so it finds level and leaks through whatever sand gaps exist. Rendering writes a per-pixel ImageData buffer with subtle hashed per-cell shading (grainy sand, shimmering water, speckled stone) for texture without sprites. A brush ring paints along a Bresenham-smoothed drag path; tools, brush size and a live census of every loose particle live in the strip below.
TECHNIQUES: bottom-up falling-sand CA with parity-flipped traversal, stochastic diagonal fall resolution and water lateral spreading, direct ImageData pixel rasterisation with per-cell hashed tint, distance-correct circular brush painting with stroke interpolation, typed-array grid state.
INTERACTION: Choose SAND, WATER, STONE or ERASE, size the brush, and draw physics — dam a lake with stone and watch water hunt the leak, pour sand into water and watch it win, or EMPTY ROOM and start the geology again.
