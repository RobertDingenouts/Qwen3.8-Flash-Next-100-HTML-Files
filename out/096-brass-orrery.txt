TITLE: The Brass Orrery — clockwork heavens, wound by hand
PROMPT: A museum-object orrery on velvet black: meshed brass gears at the base, ivory-on-black orbit rings, five invented planets on brass arms — one with a lenticular ring — all driven by a single warp dial you wind.
DESCRIPTION: Three toothed gears (drawn as alternating-radius 28-gons with hollow bosses and rims) counter-rotate in correct-ish mesh ratios below the dial plate, connected to the sun pillar by a visible rod; the sun itself is a radial glow with a candle-bright core. Five planets orbit on rings at speeds scaled from their almanac years (Aster at 0.24 through ringed Balthasar at 11.9), each shaded with a radial gradient toward the black, carried on thin brass arms from the hub. Balthasar’s ring is a rotated, vertically-squashed stroked circle that passes behind and in front convincingly enough at this scale. Clicking a planet dashes a selection halo and prints its hand-lathed provenance.
TECHNIQUES: gear rendering via alternating polar radii with phase offsets for mesh illusion, orbit timing from Kepler-flavoured year constants, planet terminator shading through offset radial gradients, squashed-circle ring composition with canvas transform stacks, pointer hit-testing on live body positions.
INTERACTION: WIND the warp slider from museum-maintenance crawl to planetary chaos, CLICK any planet for its almanac plaque, and try to spot Balthasar’s ring slipping behind its own globe.
