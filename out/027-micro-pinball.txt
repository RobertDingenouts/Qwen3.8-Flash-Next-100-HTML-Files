TITLE: Micro Pinball — a felt-green table you can actually believe
PROMPT: Squeeze the full cathedral of pinball — plunger, pop bumpers, slingshots, flippers that flick with angular momentum — into one tiny navy-and-gold table that behaves itself under real circle-versus-line physics.
DESCRIPTION: A virtual 360×620 playfield with walls built from line segments, four glowing pop bumpers, two triangular slingshots, an arc of guide walls and a plunger lane with a charge bar. The ball is a chrome marble under gravity; flippers are driven capsules whose surface velocity is transferred through relative-velocity reflection, so a well-timed flip really launches. Bumpers kick to a minimum speed and flash white; combos stack by chaining bumper hits quickly.
TECHNIQUES: circle-vs-segment collision with closest-point projection, moving-surface impulse transfer (ω×r surface velocity) for flippers, segment-polyline table construction in virtual coordinates scaled to any viewport, combo multiplier timer, one-tap launch + hold-to-charge plunger, sleep-drain detection.
INTERACTION: Hold SPACE (or tap the upper playfield) to draw the plunger and release to launch, ←/→ or A/L flick the flippers (on touch, hold the lower-left/lower-right corners), light bumpers and slings for points — three balls per game, combos for hot streaks.
