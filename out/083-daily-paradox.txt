TITLE: The Daily Paradox — all the news that’s debatable
PROMPT: A broadsheet front page where every headline is a small epistemological crime and the ink will not hold still: hover any headline and it scrambles itself into static before resigning back into its own words.
DESCRIPTION: Classic newsprint architecture — double-rule masthead (Vol. XC · No. 99, datelined from the real today, priced at one paradox), a three-column article river with bylines, hairline rules and italic footer witticisms — loaded fresh per edition from a pool of fourteen paradoxical headlines and nine interchangeable body paragraphs that absurdly match any of them. Hover triggers a scramble-decode animation that resolves left to right; hovering inverts to white-on-black for the duration. REPRINT reshuffles the whole front page like a cosmic wire service.
TECHNIQUES: randomized headline/body pairing per edition, interval-driven scramble-decode animation with fractional reveal index, inverted hover state on headline elements, responsive CSS multi-column newspaper flow with break-inside avoid, live locale dateline.
INTERACTION: Hover headlines to destabilise them (they always give in and re-form), click nothing — you’re already complicit — and hit REPRINT TODAY’S EDITION for a different set of certainties.
