TITLE: Word Ticker — flap board at gate 3
PROMPT: Recreate the clacking split-flap departure board as a typing machine: every character that changes must physically flip — tumbling through two or three nonsense glyphs before settling — and scrolling the headline should look exactly like airport hardware dreaming.
DESCRIPTION: Twenty-plus canvas flaps on a dark chassis; your message scrolls endlessly through them, and every changed letter plays a two-stage flip: the top leaf shrinks toward the hinge, then the bottom leaf unfolds showing the new glyph, with random characters flickering in the queue ahead of the real one. The whole board is hand-drawn per frame — seam line, per-leaf tinting, subtle bezel — and a RING BELL button chirps the two-tone gate chime through WebAudio while REWIND snaps the ticker back to square one.
TECHNIQUES: canvas split-flap animation with two-clip halves and scaleY hinge geometry, randomized pending-character queues per flap, modular scrolling string, DPR-aware board with adaptive column count, triangle-wave two-note chime scheduling, live input rebinding.
INTERACTION: Type anything into the message bar and the board re-clocks every letter, slide PACE from leisurely clack to rush-hour, REWIND to jump back to the start of the sentence, RING BELL for the boarding chime.
