TITLE: Klotski Box — 横刀立马, the general must leave
PROMPT: Ship the oldest sliding-block game on Earth — Huarong Pass, the 2×2 warlord boxed in by four vertical generals, a horizontal one and four footmen — as a lacquered wooden puzzle with brushstroke tiles and the gate at the bottom of the world.
DESCRIPTION: A 4×5 wooden tray holding the classic "Across the Board, Stand the Horses" rack: Cao Cao's rosewood square, jade-green vertical generals, a slate horizontal Guan Yu and four sand footmen with a single gap pair. Drag any block and it slides cell-by-cell through empty squares (multi-cell drags allowed along one axis), tiles lift with drop shadows, and the count runs against the legendary 81-move minimum. Get the warlord to the bottom-centre and he animates out through the south gate while the board congratulates you.
TECHNIQUES: occupancy collision for variable-size sliding pieces, axis-snapped multi-step pointer dragging, per-drag undo stack with move accounting, exit animation on win detection, CJK label typography on canvas with vertical two-glyph stacking, DPR-scaled tray geometry.
INTERACTION: Drag any block along empty space (it slides as far as the drag and the gaps allow), UNDO rewinds a whole drag, RE-RACK restores the 81-move classic — then walk 曹操 down to the open gate.
