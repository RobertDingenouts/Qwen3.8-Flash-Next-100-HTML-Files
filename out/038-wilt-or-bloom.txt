TITLE: Wilt or Bloom — hangman with a living greenhouse
PROMPT: Retell the letter-guessing gallows as a botanical wager: every correct letter grows a stem and a leaf, every wrong one drains colour from the petals, until either the word flowers in watercolour or its petals fall on the pot.
DESCRIPTION: A canvas terracotta pot under a hand-tied quadratic stem that grows proportionally to revealed letters; leaves sprout alternately and tilt, a watercolour bloom of animated ellipse petals crowns the top. Wrong guesses bleed sepia confetti and push the whole plant toward a drooping, browning wilt transform (hue, droop offsets, stretched petals) over seven dry days. Fourteen botanical words each arrive with a wry dictionary hint.
TECHNIQUES: quadratic-bezier stem growth with progress-driven control points, state-based wilt interpolation (colour, droop, petal stretch), falling petal particle system, per-guess reveal pass over DOM letter slots, disabled-key board with hit/miss staining, responsive botanical scene sizing.
INTERACTION: Type or tap a letter to water the plant; correct letters reveal in the word and grow the stem, wrong ones drain it — flower the word before the petals drop, then PLANT ANOTHER for a fresh specimen.
