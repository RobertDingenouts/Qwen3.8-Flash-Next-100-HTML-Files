TITLE: Dice Tray — ivory, walnut, and the old quarrel with chance
PROMPT: A felt-and-walnut dice tray with real table physics-lite: grab dice, whip them, watch polygonal ivories collide, settle, and confess their numbers one by one while the tray sums the conspiracy.
DESCRIPTION: The page itself is the walnut (banded gradient grain with an inset tray rim casting inner shadow); on the canvas live up to fourteen dice — d4 triangles, d6 squares, d8 lozenges, d12 pentagons, d20 hexagons — each with an ivory radial sheen and a serif pip numeral. Dice are soft circles for collision (walls damp at 0.7, mutual impulses split along the contact normal) but read as polygons; the number counter-rotates slightly against the body so tumbling flickers while rolling and snaps upright at rest. Drag-throw gives velocity from the last pointer delta plus a spin; ROLL ALL shakes the tray; the Σ readout shows an ellipsis until the last die settles, then the honest total.
TECHNIQUES: polygonal die rendering with counter-rotating face numerals, circle-based pairwise impulse collision with wall damping and drag-throw velocity injection, settle detection driving UI state, wood-grain via layered repeating gradients with inset shadow tray.
INTERACTION: Add dice d4 through d20, DRAG-FLICK them like a nervous player, ROLL ALL for a volley, tap a resting die to re-roll it, SWEEP TRAY to clear the table — and the Σ waits for the last die like a croupier.
