TITLE: Glass Tank — click to feed, watch, breathe
PROMPT: A zen aquarium rendered entirely in one canvas loop: procedural fish with wagging tails and dorsal fins, swaying kelp, rising bubbles, drifting light shafts — and hunger that makes nine idle wanderers suddenly agree on a decision.
DESCRIPTION: A sand-plinth-framed glass plate: graded aqua water with a bright surface band, dune-curved sand bed, nine kelp strands swaying on individual phases, and a steady stream of wobbling bubbles. The fish are oval bodies with triangle tails wagged by a sine phase, dorsal fins, and tiny dark eyes, each tinted from a small hue deck; they wander with gentle noise-ish steering and wall bounces. Click anywhere above the sand and three flakes sink on wobbling paths — every fish within sight re-targets the nearest flake, converges, and eats it (slow, visible growth up to a cap), then drifts back to ambience.
TECHNIQUES: agent steering with weighted wander and nearest-target pursuit, canvas creature drawing with phase-driven tail articulation, particle systems for bubbles and sinking flakes with lifetime filtering, sand silhouette from a sampled sine ridge, drifting specular surface highlights.
INTERACTION: Click the water to drop food — the tank reorganises around your crumbs, fish fatten imperceptibly over long sessions, and the population counter keeps the census honest.
