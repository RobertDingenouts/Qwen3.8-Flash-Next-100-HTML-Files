TITLE: Pendulum Wave — harmonics of a chalkboard swing
PROMPT: Stage the classic physics-demo snake as a chalkboard ballet: thirty-some pendulums whose lengths are tuned to whole-number frequencies, so the line snakes, splits, scatters into noise and mysteriously resynchronises on cue.
DESCRIPTION: A row of glowing bobs hung from a rail, each swinging at (8+i)/(cycle/2) cycles per second — lengths grow geometrically toward the right so the wave coheres. An amber contour thread links the bobs into the signature snake; a slowly decaying particle layer leaves phosphorescent trails; a live phase meter counts toward the grand re-alignment. Bobs can be yanked off-phase mid-performance and drift back.
TECHNIQUES: frequency-ratio pendulum physics with analytic (damped-cosine) solving instead of integration, offscreen trail canvas with alpha-decay persistence, geometric length spacing, pointer drag converting to angular offset relative to the analytic state, hue-spread bob palette with glow, DPR-scaled dual-canvas compositing.
INTERACTION: Watch the phase % climb toward 100 and the flock snap back into one line, RELEASE to restart the cycle, slide BOBS and CYCLE to re-tune the ensemble, and drag any bob to teach it rebellion (it settles back).
