TITLE: Truchet Wanderer — the maze that rearranges itself
PROMPT: Build a maze that will not sit still: a field of rotating quarter-circle tiles that a small glowing explorer keeps relearning, where the visitor flips single tiles with a finger and watches the whole route redraw.
DESCRIPTION: A Truchet arc-tile field (two states per tile, drawn as corner quarter-arcs) with a path-tracing engine that walks edge-to-edge connections from the left border and samples the arcs into a polyline. A hot-orange explorer with a fading comet tail runs the route while a faint underlay shows the full curve. Tiles animate a 90° flip; the engine recomputes the route after every flip and even tries several entry rows to find a long, winding trace.
TECHNIQUES: arc/edge connection algebra for Truchet topology, polyline sampling of quarter-arcs with corner-relative parametrisation, eased tile-rotation tweening, multi-entry path search, DPR-scaled canvas with glow-shadow trail rendering, brutalist Helvetica furniture.
INTERACTION: Click/tap any tile to flip it and rewire the maze live, FLIP TWELVE to shake up the whole field, RESEED for fresh randomness, PACE slider to speed the explorer up to a sprint.
