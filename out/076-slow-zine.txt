TITLE: Slow Zine — issue one, two inks and one opinion
PROMPT: A risograph zine that exists only as ink logic: procedurally halftoned red/blue plate art, foldable spreads that turn with a hinge, and manifesto copy arguing, in two colours, for slowness against the feed.
DESCRIPTION: Three double-page spreads on cream stock, bound with CSS perspective: turning peels the right-hand sheet away on a left-origin 3D hinge. Each spread carries a canvas halftone plate generated as offset red and blue rectangles over three procedural graphics — a sun over a horizon band, a sine interference wave, a rain-streak field — multiplied into the paper with mix-blend-mode and rendered pixelated like a true low-DPI riso print. Typography is Courier grit: big two-ink display headings, dashed column rules, page numbers with tracked-out letterspacing. The GRAIN slider re-quantises every plate live, from nearly-photographic to pure newspaper lie.
TECHNIQUES: procedural halftone plate synthesis (gradient-sampled value fields → offset dual-ink rectangle dots), mix-blend multiply print simulation, CSS 3D page-turn with z-index choreography, keyboard arrow navigation, live re-render of plate art from a grain control.
INTERACTION: TURN the pages (or use arrow keys), drag GRAIN to move between photo-print and dot-confession, and read the manifesto at whatever slow pace you can afford.
