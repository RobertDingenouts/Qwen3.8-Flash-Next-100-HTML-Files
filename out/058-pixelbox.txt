TITLE: Pixelbox — 32×32 pocket studio
PROMPT: A paint program the size of a game console: thirty-two by thirty-two honest pixels, sixteen hand-chosen pigments, pencil, bucket, dropper and undo — and the quiet courtesy of remembering your tiny masterpiece after a refresh.
DESCRIPTION: A chunky canvas studio where every cell is a real pixel drawn crisp at any zoom: pencil paints along a Bresenham drag path so strokes never gap, the bucket does click-flood fills, the eyedropper steals colours into the palette selection, and right-drag erases without leaving the pencil. A 24-step undo ring, full-clear, and PNG export that bakes the grid at ×8 nearest-neighbour scale. The board autosaves itself to local storage between visits.
TECHNIQUES: grid-backed image model with DPR-aware pixel-snapped rendering, Bresenham line interpolation for drag painting, queue-based flood fill, pointer-button erase handling with tool restoration, localStorage persistence, offscreen nearest-neighbour PNG bake and download.
INTERACTION: Drag to draw, right-drag to rub out, BUCKET for areas, PICK to steal a colour, click a palette chip to choose pigment, UNDO peels strokes back, CLEAR empties the sheet, and SAVE PNG hands your pixels to the world at eight-times glory.
