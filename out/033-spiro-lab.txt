TITLE: Spiro Lab — a rolling-circle atelier
PROMPT: Run a Victorian brass instrument for making curves: two numbered discs, one pen offset, and the machine quietly announces when the curve will close — every finished figure gets stamped into a collector's strip.
DESCRIPTION: A spirograph bench with a cream plotting plate and a brass instrument panel. Sliders drive R (ring), r (pinion) and d (pen offset); the hypotrochoid is plotted live in translucent hue-mixed ink at hundreds of micro-segments per frame until it closes, after it holds, fades and re-plots. The note line computes the real closure math (r/gcd turns, R/gcd spokes) in number theory, and closed figures are photographed into a thumbnail strip of up to six keepsakes.
TECHNIQUES: parametric hypotrochoid plotting with pen offset, gcd-based closure detection for exact period length, incremental segment ink with oscillating lightness, canvas-to-thumbnail stamping, number-theory captioning, responsive bench layout (panel collapses under the plate on phones).
INTERACTION: Slide R, r, d to morph the machine live, shift HUE for inky colour, press one of five brass presets (rose 54, galaxy, sunburst, woven, lace), watch each closed figure stamp itself into the collector's strip.
