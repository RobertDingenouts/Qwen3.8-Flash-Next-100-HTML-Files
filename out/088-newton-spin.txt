TITLE: Newton Spin — paint the disc, spin it, catch the white lie
PROMPT: Newton’s colour top as a craft object: hand-paint arcs of primary pigment onto an ivory disc, wind up the spin, and watch red, green and blue conspire into a colour that isn’t there — additive grey dressed as white.
DESCRIPTION: A soft-shadowed white disc on paper stock; dragging around its hub smears brush-width wedges of the chosen paint, merging same-colour neighbours into one arc and trimming an unruly history. The SPIN dial winds the disc from meditative to helicopter — and at speed, opposite arcs average under your eye into muddy, glorious off-white, the eighteenth-century parlor trick rendered at sixty frames. Full spectrum paints six equal sectors of ROYGBIV automatically; White disc returns the blank; the pivot cross at the centre keeps the illusion honest.
TECHNIQUES: polar brush painting with angular delta and wrap correction, arc-segment merging and history trimming, canvas clipping to a disc with per-frame rotation transform, drop-shadow disc staging, neighbour-detection painting state.
INTERACTION: Drag around the disc to paint (any colour from the eight-chip deck), crank SPIN until the pigments agree on grey, AUTO for the full spectrum disc, and WHITE to smear it all away and do Newton’s homework again.
