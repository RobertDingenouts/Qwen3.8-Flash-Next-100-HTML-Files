TITLE: Thread Loom — verlet fabric you can hurt
PROMPT: Give the visitor a loom of knitted light — four textile presets woven from spring constraints — where one toggle turns the cursor from a hand into shears and the fabric keeps its grief (dragged points remember velocity, cut threads stop existing).
DESCRIPTION: A verlet-integration playground on slate: point masses with position-history, distance constraints solved over three iterations, and gravity. Handkerchief, rope bridge, banner and curtain presets pin different topologies; threads change thickness with stretch so strain is visible; pinned anchor nodes glow as gold rivets. SHEARS mode line-segment-tests the cursor's stroke against every constraint and severs it.
TECHNIQUES: verlet integration with velocity-from-position-history, iterative constraint relaxation, drag-with-velocity by back-setting previous positions, segment-segment distance for stroke cutting, stretch-driven stroke width, pinned-node rendering, responsive lattice rebuild on resize.
INTERACTION: Grab and fling any node of the fabric (release velocity carries), flip to SHEARS and slash through threads to cut the cloth, switch presets (handkerchief / rope bridge / banner / curtain), RE-TIE rebuilds whatever survived.
