TITLE: Simon Halo — repeat the light
PROMPT: Rebuild the memory toy as a halo of eight pastel neon arcs floating in charcoal — the only UI is the ring itself, a big number in the middle and a whisper of status text, and failure should feel like a lovely thing breaking.
DESCRIPTION: A canvas ring of eight glowing segments, each tuned to a pentatonic frequency through WebAudio so every press sings. Sequences grow one note per round and tempo creeps up with the level; playback strobes arcs with bloom shadow, input echoes with inner spark dots. Failure scrambles the halo in a strobing confetti of flashes and a low sub-tone, keeping a best-round counter; an idle orbiting tick marks the halo as waiting.
TECHNIQUES: annular sector path drawing for the eight arcs, pointer polar-coordinate hit testing, oscillator envelopes scheduled on the audio clock, state machine (idle/show/input/wait/fail) with chained timeouts, speed ramping by level, DPR ring sizing.
INTERACTION: Press START, watch the halo play its growing tune, then repeat it by clicking the arcs in order — each correct round adds a step and tightens the tempo; one wrong arc and the halo shatters; best round survives on the status line.
