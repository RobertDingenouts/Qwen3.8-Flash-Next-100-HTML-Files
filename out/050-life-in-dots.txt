TITLE: Life in Dots — four rules, no mercy
PROMPT: Conway’s Garden of Eden as a dot matrix observatory: cells rendered as glowing pips with soft haloes on a faint graph, where population is a live sparkline and the classics (Gosper’s gun, the pulsar, r-pentomino) come pre-planted for your convenience.
DESCRIPTION: A wrapped toroidal Life world of ~130×80 cells updated at a controllable frame rate. Living cells glow as bright pips inside translucent haloes; the population history draws a rolling oscilloscope trace in the dock. Drag to paint or erase with the brush taking its mode from the first touched cell. Pre-stamp buttons drop a verified Gosper glider gun, a period-3 pulsar built by vertical mirror of its top half, or the infamous r-pentomino; SEED CHAOS fills 22% at random, VOID burns it all down.
TECHNIQUES: double-buffered toroidal Life update with modular neighbour indexing, glow rendering via double-arc haloes, population sparkline with autoscale, paint-brush mode inference on first contact, classic-pattern encoding (coordinate lists + mirrored row strings), grid-resizing state copy.
INTERACTION: Draw and erase with the mouse (it copies the vibe of the first cell you touch), PAUSE to freeze and edit, SPEED to choose your tempo, plant the gun/pulsar/r-pentomino or chaos, and watch GEN/POP plus the sparkline chart the rise and fall of civilizations.
