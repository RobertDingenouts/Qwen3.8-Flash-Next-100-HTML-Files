TITLE: Rule Machine — a cellular automata tape printer
PROMPT: Build a printing press for Wolfram's 1-D cellular automata: an endless paper tape scrolls upward, one generation per heartbeat, with the visitor spinning the 256-bit rule dial live so patterns mutate mid-tape, in ink or in eight-colour "opus" mode.
DESCRIPTION: A monospace control console pinned to the paper. A Uint8Array world of up to ~500 cells advances every tick and the canvas scroll-tricks (drawImage shift) push the tape up forever. Changing the rule number with arrows, keys or preset stamps transforms history instantly; opus colouring paints each live cell by its 3-bit neighbourhood pattern. Clicking the tape scars a fresh random seed line across its path.
TECHNIQUES: bitwise rule extraction ((rule>>pattern)&1), edge wrap modes, drawImage-based canvas scrolling under dual transform states, DPR-aware cell grid, palette indexed by neighbourhood id, keyboard + stepper UI in a print-shop monospace aesthetic.
INTERACTION: Arrow keys or ▲▼ step the rule 0–255, preset buttons stamp famous rules (30, 90, 110, 184…), feed slider sets tape speed, opus colour and wrap toggles, RESEED/SINGLE-CELL buttons, click anywhere on the tape to scar a new seed row.
