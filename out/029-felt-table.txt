TITLE: Felt Table — a one-player pool story
PROMPT: Give a billiards parlour to a single visitor: aim down a dashed sight-line, let the power build while you hold your breath, and clear six coloured stones before daring the black — sink it early and the table wins.
DESCRIPTION: A 800×420 virtual felt with mahogany rails, six pockets, a racked sextet plus the eight-ball and an ivory cue ball. Aiming is cursor-following; holding the pointer charges a green-to-red power bar and pulls the cue stick back; release strikes. Elastic equal-mass collisions, cushion restitution, pocket capture, scratch respot, and a small rulebook: pot all six colours first, then the eight, or the table is yours.
TECHNIQUES: impulse-based equal-mass ball collisions along contact normals, cushion boundary reflection with loss, radial-gradient ball shading with offset specular and drop shadows, one-way power-bar charge loop, head-spot respot collision check, felt gradient and wood frame compositing, virtual-coordinate scaling.
INTERACTION: Move to aim, press and hold to draw the cue (watch the power bar turn from green to red), release to strike; RE-RACK resets the diamond — clear all six numbered balls, then call your shot on the eight.
