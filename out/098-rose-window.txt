TITLE: Rose Window — consecrated geometry, random glazier
PROMPT: A Gothic rose window generated by a lazy divine glazier: hub, three arc rings, a machicolation of dark outer voussoirs, jewel-tone fills with lead came thick enough to hear, and a daylight slider that decides whether it’s Vespers or noon.
DESCRIPTION: Polar geometry in four rings on a violet-dark plate: a central roundel, a narrow petal ring, a doubled mid ring, and an outer lobe ring — some arcs cut as round oculus cells — all stroked in fat near-black lead lines over eight jewel hues blended through a radial gradient toward shadow so the glass deepens outward. DAYLIGHT scales every pane’s alpha from candlelit crypt to blazing clerestory, thickens the gold halo around the window, and pours a trapezoid of coloured light onto the nave floor. Clicking the window or hitting RECUT sends a new seed through the glazier’s lot, and the PETALS dial re-traces the whole rose at six-to-sixteen-fold symmetry.
TECHNIQUES: layered polar arc-segment composition with ringed annulus paths, seeded jewel assignment over a fixed palette, radial-gradient glass shading and global-alpha daylight modulation, projected light shaft via clipped triangle gradient, live geometric regeneration from a petal-count control.
INTERACTION: Slide DAYLIGHT to time-of-day the window, PETALS to re-flower the rose, and CLICK THE GLASS (or RECUT) whenever the saints need a new colour scheme.
