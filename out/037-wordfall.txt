TITLE: Wordfall — a tiny dictionary, six planted guesses
PROMPT: A Wordle for a reading room: small serif dictionary, sage-and-clay palette, and every guess falls onto the board like a domino row — tiles flip in sequence, letter keys re-stain, and the dictionary is just honest about being small.
DESCRIPTION: A full Wordle implementation in newspaper-serif styling with an embedded curated vocabulary (~110 five-letter words doubling as answer pool and whitelist), correct duplicate-letter two-pass scoring (greens first, then ambers against unused letters), sequential tile flip animations, shake rejection for unknown words, and a keyboard that permanently remembers the worst-known state of each letter.
TECHNIQUES: two-pass Wordle scoring with a used-letter mask, async per-tile flip animation chained via recursion, DOM grid/keyboard generation, physical-keyboard + on-screen parity, class-priority updates (green > amber > grey) on keys, responsive min() sizing for phones.
INTERACTION: Type or tap letters to build a guess, ENTER plants it (the board shakes for non-words, backspace edits), sink all six rows to lose, guess the secret word to close the book — NEW WORD starts a fresh volume.
