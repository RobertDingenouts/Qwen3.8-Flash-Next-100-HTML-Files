TITLE: World Strip — the planet, already awake or still asleep
PROMPT: One honest scroll of time zones: every city a row, every row a painted day-arc from midnight to midnight, the sun pinned exactly where that sky currently is — and your home city underlined in gold so you can see who you are bothering at 3am.
DESCRIPTION: Eighteen cities, from Reykjavík to Auckland, each rendered as a live clock card: monospace local time, a horizontal sky-strip gradient (deep night, violet dawn, amber sunrise, blue day, sunset, night) and a glowing sun — which turns into a cooler moon after dusk — sliding along the arc by fraction of local day. Clicking any city re-anchors the whole list: offsets in hours and minutes are recomputed against that home port, and the row glows gold. All clock faces come from real Intl timezone data, refreshed every fifteen seconds.
TECHNIQUES: Intl.DateTimeFormat formatToParts for true time-zone local time (DST included), pseudo-moment reconstruction for pairwise offset maths, CSS gradient sky arcs with percentage-positioned celestial markers, home-anchored re-render, live self-updating clock list.
INTERACTION: Click any city to make it home — offsets flip to “what time is it there from here”, the suns keep crawling, and the header keeps your own machine honest.
