TITLE: Fractal Seasons — one tree, four selves
PROMPT: Compress a year into a slider: the same fractal tree must blossom pink, leaf out green, burn orange and drop its leaves, then stand bare in snow, with wind combing through every branch at every moment.
DESCRIPTION: A recursive binary/ternary fractal tree regrown from a random seed, re-simulated every frame so branches flex with layered sine wind whose amplitude depends on depth and season. A "the year" slider blends sky gradient, ground colour, leaf hue and opacity between spring, summer, autumn and winter; autumn detaches actual falling leaves that tumble across the canvas, winter grows snow caps on twigs and drifts flakes.
TECHNIQUES: seeded PRNG-driven recursive L-branching drawn fresh each frame (deterministic wobble), per-depth wind phase offsets, piecewise linear seasonal colour interpolation, particle system for detached leaves and snow, Verdana lab-chrome UI over a painterly canvas.
INTERACTION: Drag THE YEAR to scrub through the seasons and watch the canopy thin or flush, press GROW A NEW SEED for a different tree, watch autumn leaves peel off mid-drag and snow settle in winter.
