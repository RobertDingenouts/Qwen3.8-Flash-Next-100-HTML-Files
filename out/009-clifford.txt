TITLE: Strange Weather — a Clifford attractor observatory
PROMPT: Put four numbers on sliders and let them become weather: every frame, twenty-eight thousand point-ghosts fall through the Clifford map and pile into galaxies, blooms and filaments, drifting on forever unless the visitor grabs the dials.
DESCRIPTION: A literary-named attractor observatory. The Clifford iterated map x' = sin(a·y)+c·cos(a·x), y' = sin(b·x)+d·cos(b·y) is plotted as translucent micro-points accumulating on a slowly fading black field; hue creeps through the spectrum over time. Sliders ease toward their targets so the nebula morphs organically; a drift mode wobbles all four constants on slow sinusoids on its own.
TECHNIQUES: iterative strange-attractor plotting with persistence-fade compositing, target-eased parameter system shared between sliders and drift oscillators, 28k fillRect splats per rAF at DPR scale, hue-cycling alpha palette, serif-title / monospace-instrument type pairing, blend-mode screen overlay text.
INTERACTION: Drag a, b, c, d to melt the attractor into a new shape, press RANDOMIZE or a named state (BLOOM, FILAMENT, WHORL) to travel there smoothly, and toggle DRIFT+ to let the constants wander on their own.
