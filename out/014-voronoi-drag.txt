TITLE: Cell Kingdom — a draggable poster Voronoi
PROMPT: Make territory physical: paint the screen as a bold risograph map of territories that each recompute the instant you drag one of their seeds, with fat black borders like ink rules and three whole palettes to switch regimes.
DESCRIPTION: A live pixel-classified Voronoi diagram: every coarse render-pixel finds its nearest of ~18 wandering seeds, gets that seed's poster colour with distance shading, and darkens where the two nearest seeds compete to form thick keyline borders. Seeds drift with a gentle breathing motion, can be grabbed and thrown around, and double-tapping the map plants new territory.
TECHNIQUES: brute-force nearest/second-nearest scan per low-res pixel with sqrt only at the edge test, ImageData LUT-free direct RGB writes, SC-scaled offscreen buffer with nearest-neighbour upscale for print texture, pointer-capture seed dragging with grab/grabbing cursor states, hard-light blended Arial Black titling.
INTERACTION: Grab any black seed dot and drag the map into new shapes, double-tap/click to plant a new seed, +/− SEED to change the population, and cycle three curated PALETTE regimes (sunburn, electric, museum).
