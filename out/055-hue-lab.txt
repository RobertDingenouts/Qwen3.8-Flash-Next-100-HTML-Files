TITLE: Hue Lab — pick a colour, interrogate it
PROMPT: Build a colour wheel as a courtroom: every hue the visitor chooses must immediately confess its hex, its friends (complement, triad, analogue), its family portrait in tints, and whether white or black text may safely stand on it.
DESCRIPTION: A full-circle HSL wheel painted per-pixel — hue spinning around, saturation running outward, lightness held by a slider — with a draggable target that picks as you sweep. The chosen colour balloons into a big hex chip (tap to copy), and four scheme rows re-render instantly: complement with a softened partner, triad, analogous fan, and a tint ladder. A contrast line computes WCAG luminance ratios against pure white and pure black and rules on AA safety in bold.
TECHNIQUES: pixel-wise ImageData hue-saturation disc rendering with anti-aliased rim, hue/sat polar picking from pointer geometry, accurate HSL↔RGB conversion in both directions, WCAG relative-luminance contrast maths, DOM swatch factories with click behaviours, DPR-correct wheel sizing.
INTERACTION: Click-drag anywhere on the wheel to hunt a colour (radius = saturation, angle = hue), slide LIGHTNESS to darken the whole world, tap any swatch to inspect it, and tap the big hex to copy it to the clipboard.
