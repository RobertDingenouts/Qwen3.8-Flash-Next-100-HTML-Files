TITLE: Menu Néant — dinner for the beautifully undecided
PROMPT: A Michelin-of-the-void menu in black and gold Didot: pretentious nothing served as dishes, each orderable, totalling onto a persistent bill bar that judges your table with typographic patience.
DESCRIPTION: A midnight dining room with tracked-out section heads (Pour Commencer: against appetite; Douceur Finale: sweet, eventually) over dotted-ruled dishes — name in serif, description in tracked-out monospace caps, prices in gold — including Bread, Absent at €0 and Water, Filtered with judgment included. Clicking a dish strikes gold through its name (✦), appends it to the sticky bill bar with running total, and prints your whole indulgence as a dot-separated marquee. CLEAR TABLE resets the shame.
TECHNIQUES: data-driven menu rendering from nested section/item tables, added-state guard with Set-based idempotent ordering, sticky bill bar with live total and overflow-ellipsis order line, two-typeface hierarchy (display serif + monospace metadata) as the entire visual system, responsive single-column collapse.
INTERACTION: Click dishes onto the bill bar — the €0 Bread, Absent is always correct — watch the total climb with your delusions, and CLEAR TABLE whenever you regain your senses.
