TITLE: Vector Storm — asteroids with a glowing edge
PROMPT: Return to the vector era: white wireframe rocks tumbling out of a phosphor haze, a triangle ship that drifts like a sled, and every destruction a small firework of points on glass.
DESCRIPTION: Full-asteroids arcade physics rendered as glowing line art behind a CRT vignette: procedurally lumpy wireframe rocks that split twice, thrust particles venting from the engine nozzle, shot streaks with motion tails, and point-explosions when anything dies. Ship inertia, screen-wrap on all axes, invulnerability blink after respawn, and escalating waves — each clear washes the screen in a warp flash.
TECHNIQUES: polygon rock generation from radius jitter arrays, momentum-plus-drag flight model with speed clamp, recursive rock splitting, particle pooling with decay lifetimes, alpha-fill frame clearing for phosphor trails without full persistence, split-screen touch zones for thrust/turn/fire, keyboard input map.
INTERACTION: Arrows/WASD to turn and thrust, SPACE to fire (hold ok), on touch hold the bottom band left/right/middle for turn and thrust while tapping the field to shoot — survive the storm; three hulls, endless waves, auto-reboot on last life.
