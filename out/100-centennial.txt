TITLE: Centennial — one hundred little machines, all still running
PROMPT: The final page of the collection must be about the collection itself: a hundred-tile mosaic where every tile is one of its siblings, confetti for the count-up headline, and a readout that lets each page whisper its last will and testament.
DESCRIPTION: The masthead pops in letter-by-letter — ONE HUNDRED in rotating hues, each glyph scaling down from zero on a 70ms delay — while a canvas confetti storm fires from the centre with gravity, drag, tumbling rectangles and a 2.2-second life fade. Below, a responsive auto-fill grid lays all 100 numbered titles as cards: seeded hues with a dark tile every few beats, micro-rotations for a pinned-to-a-corkboard sprawl, and a springy hover that lifts, un-rotates and scales each sibling. Clicking a tile rings it in black and rotates through five epitaphs; the tiny 6.5px type at 360px is the whole collection squeezed through a letterbox.
TECHNIQUES: per-character entrance choreography with staggered CSS keyframes, full-viewport confetti particle system with rotation and life-fade scheduling, deterministic per-tile hue/rotation hashing for organic mosaic layouts, auto-fill grid responsive reflow with legibility floor, self-referential catalogue rendering.
INTERACTION: Watch the letters pop and the confetti fall on arrival, CLICK any of the ninety-nine siblings to summon its epitaph, and hit CONFETTI AGAIN whenever one hundred pages deserve another round.
