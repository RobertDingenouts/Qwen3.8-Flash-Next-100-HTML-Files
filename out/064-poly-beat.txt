TITLE: Poly Beat — rings that refuse to agree
PROMPT: Build a polyrhythm machine as brass clockwork: concentric rings each ticking their own subdivision against one heartbeat, every crossing of the twelve-o’clock line a soft pentatonic bell, and a metronome pendulum keeping everyone honest at the bottom.
DESCRIPTION: Up to six orbiting dot-machines on black: ring i travels ×d subdivisions per beat, flashes and strikes a pentatonic note (triangle-wave bell, louder for inner voices) exactly when its dot passes north — so 4 against 5 against 6 blooms into phasing chords. Beat ticks are marked by a swinging pendulum bob; ring dots flare white at the strike; tick marks on each ring show where its family will land; the selected ring brightens and is steered by the RING BEATS slider.
TECHNIQUES: continuous phase accumulator decoupled from BPM with wrap-detection for strike events, pentatonic scheduling on the audio clock, selected-ring focus model with polar hit-testing, flash envelopes from circular phase distance, pendulum driven by the shared beat clock.
INTERACTION: Click a ring to tune it and slide RING BEATS from 2 to 12, +/− RINGS to stack the argument up to six voices, BPM to choose your heartbeat, PAUSE to freeze the pattern mid-phenomenon, SOUND to mime it silently.
