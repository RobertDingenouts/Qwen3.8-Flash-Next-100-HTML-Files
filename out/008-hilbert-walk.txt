TITLE: Hilbert Walk — a turtle tracing space-filling curves
PROMPT: Choreograph mathematics: let a luminous turtle walk the Hilbert curve at increasing depths, each segment tinted across a spectrum, so the visitor literally watches a one-dimensional line learn to fill a plane.
DESCRIPTION: A dark gallery wall with a letterboxed square canvas. A hand-written bit-twiddling d2xy mapper converts index to grid coordinates, and a glowing white head drags a rainbow-dyed polyline through every cell in Hilbert order. Depth 1 to 7 means 4 to 16384 segments; clicking a cell teleports the walker there; gait slider sets walking speed. A per-segment hue gradient makes the ordering itself visible.
TECHNIQUES: Hilbert curve index mapping via the classic rx/ry rotation loop, incremental polyline animation with full-canvas redraw, DPR-crisp letterboxed canvas layout, hue-cycling stroke palette, click-to-index scan for navigation, monospace status line against geometric caps.
INTERACTION: + / − DEPTH to fold the plane tighter (watch 16k segments crawl), drag the gait slider to gallop or tiptoe, REWALK to start the traversal fresh, and tap any cell to jump the walker to that point in the ordering.
