TITLE: R Squared — drag the data until the line forgives you
PROMPT: A least-squares toy with a conscience: scatter points you can physically drag across graph paper, a dashed trend line recomputing every frame, and a big red r² that judges your relationship with the data in real time.
DESCRIPTION: On a blue graph-paper plate (CSS repeating-gradient grid under a transparent canvas), a cloud of navy points with chalky halos obeys the pointer. The moment three points exist, the page fits a true least-squares line, draws it dashed, and reports r², Pearson r with sign, and the fitted equation ŷ = a + bx in monospace. Flip RESIDUALS to swap the dashes for the line and the fit becomes solid while red vertical threads show every error term live. MAKE IT LINE UP seeds near-perfect correlation; MAKE IT CHAOS scatters a fresh null hypothesis; double-click anywhere to place a new witness.
TECHNIQUES: incremental least-squares regression with covariance sums, r² from SSres/SStot and Pearson r from the same moments, grab-and-clamp point dragging with hit-test priority, residual thread rendering bound to the fitted line, seeded correlation-scaled point clouds.
INTERACTION: Drag any point and watch r² gasp, double-click to add points, RESIDUALS to see the errors you’re making, TOGGLE the trend line, and chase 0.99+ before the page calls you a conspiracy theorist.
