TITLE: Year Garden — every day of this year, quietly grown
PROMPT: Replace the GitHub-style calendar heatmap with something alive: past days become leaves whose colour, size and vigour are grown from each date’s own seed, today wears a gold ring, and the future waits as pale empty soil.
DESCRIPTION: A 12×31 grid of plantable cells labelled by month; every past day of the current year gets deterministic colour from a seeded hash of its day-of-year — leaf-greens in hue, saturation and lightness keyed to its “health”, with the dot physically smaller for frail days. The current date is ringed in glowing gold; future days sit as hollow outlines waiting to be filled. Hovering any grown day reads its almanac entry: a seeded pair like “the drizzly lantern” or “a stubborn sparrow”. The footer sums the bloom.
TECHNIQUES: mulberry-style seeded hashing for per-day procedural attributes, day-of-year math with leap handling, month-length grid layout with void padding, hover tooltip generation from parallel word banks, gold focus ring for “today” detection.
INTERACTION: Hover (or tap) any grown day for its almanac line, watch the gold ring creep rightward on its own, and read how much of this year’s garden is already in bloom.
