TITLE: Circadian Arc — twenty-four hours in one canvas
PROMPT: Compress the whole day into a single window: a sun that must actually climb an arc, a moon keeping the opposite watch, and a sky that cannot fake the exact embarrassment of 6:40am — scrub it all with your thumb.
DESCRIPTION: A full-screen living landscape whose entire mood is a pure function of the clock: nine sky keyframes crossfade in RGB along the hours, a warm glow and rotating rays mark the low sun, stars fade in by a nightness curve with individual twinkle phases, and two silhouette hills darken with the light. A crescent-bitten moon arcs westward after dusk. Readouts name the human moment — dawn, golden hour, deep night — while a minute slider lets you replay any hour of your existence.
TECHNIQUES: piecewise linear sky-gradient interpolation across keyframes, parametric sun/moon arc positioning, nightness-driven star and terrain darkening with per-element phase, ray animation keyed to solar altitude, live-versus-manual time state with slider scrubbing.
INTERACTION: Drag SCRUB TIME to wind the day forward or back through dawn, noon and midnight — the whole world agrees — and WATCH LIVE to surrender back to real time.
