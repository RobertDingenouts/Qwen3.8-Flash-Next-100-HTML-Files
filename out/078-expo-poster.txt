TITLE: Expo Poster — Weimar geometry, remixable
PROMPT: A Bauhaus exhibition poster that refuses to sit still: every rectangle, arc and quarter-circle placed by a seeded generator, steerable live through density, circular bias and diagonal tilt, in four period-honest two-colour-plus palettes.
DESCRIPTION: A portrait poster canvas on a dim studio wall: a grid of cells each rolls dice against the seed — filled squares (rotated by the tilt dial), circles whose probability rises with CIRCLE DOMINANCE, quarter-disc swoops built from quadratic curves, and thick diagonal strokes — then a white banner band slashes across the middle carrying STAATLICHES BAUHAUS, with 1923 exhibition footers pinned bottom-left and right. Four palette chips (Bauhaus, De Stijl, Constructivist, Weimar Night) recolour everything instantly; NEW POSTER rerolls all four controls.
TECHNIQUES: seeded PRNG-driven generative grid composition, parameter-reactive redraw across four shape grammars, period poster typography banding with rotation and inverted knockout, palette-switch architecture over a shared colour index, responsive poster aspect sizing.
INTERACTION: Turn SEED like a radio dial through forty compositions, push DENSITY from sparse Constructivist to maximal, drag CIRCLE DOMINANCE to choose between square dictators and round democrats, tilt the whole era with DIAGONAL, swap palettes, and NEW POSTER for the whole reroll.
