TITLE: Haiku Breeze — the field answers when you touch it
PROMPT: A washi-paper garden that composes small poems out of controlled word-banks and moves grass under a wandering wind — click and the sky drops petals that drift with the same breeze the reeds obey.
DESCRIPTION: Sumi-green blades rise from the bottom of a full-screen canvas, each a quadratic curve swayed by a shared wind value that itself drifts on two slow sines (plus per-blade flutter), under a low ground haze and a wandering sun mist. The poem arrives line by line under a two-second fade: a 5-7-5-shaped construction from three curated banks — adjective pairs, seven-syllable phrases, and closing observations — sealed with a red 風 stamp after the last line. ANOTHER BREEZE gusts the field and writes again; any click releases six drifting petals governed by the same wind.
TECHNIQUES: wind field shared across blade curves, petals, and gust impulses with inertia smoothing; procedural haiku assembly from constraint word-banks; staged line reveal with CSS opacity transitions and seal stamp; wandering radial sun-mist; DPR grass field.
INTERACTION: CLICK the field to scatter petals into the current gust, press ANOTHER BREEZE when this poem has worn out its welcome, and watch the wind itself change its mind every few seconds.
