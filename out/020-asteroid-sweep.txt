TITLE: Asteroid Sweep — minesweeper on a CRT radar
PROMPT: Dress the oldest puzzle in computing as a 1982 orbital defence console: one phosphor green, drifting scanlines, asteroid icons instead of bombs, and every number glowing with beam bloom on black glass.
DESCRIPTION: A full minesweeper implementation framed as a radar sweep — unrevealed cells are noisy radar tiles, clearings flood-reveal in cascades, asteroids are drawn as lumpy amber rocks with bloom, and the board lives inside a bezel on a vignette-and-scanline CRT. First click is guaranteed safe (mines are planted after it), flags are kept by long-press, right-click, or FLAG mode, and a blinking terminal overlay reports SECTOR CLEAR or SWEEP FAILED.
TECHNIQUES: flood-fill zero-clearing and first-click-safe mine placement, adjacency counting pass, CSS repeating-gradient scanlines + radial vignette overlay stack, canvas glow via shadowBlur, DPR-scaled board centring, long-press vs tap disambiguation with pointer timers, monochrome phosphor UI design.
INTERACTION: Tap/click to scan cells, right-click or long-press to plant a flag, MODE button swaps between SCAN and FLAG for touch, NEW SWEEP rolls a fresh sector — flag all asteroids without touching one.
