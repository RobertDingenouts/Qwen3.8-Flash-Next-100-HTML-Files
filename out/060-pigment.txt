TITLE: Pigment — three jars, subtractive truth
PROMPT: Teach subtractive colour by kidnapping: show one impossible-looking mixed hue and surrender only cyan, magenta and yellow to the visitor — match within three percent and the studio applauds and reveals the hidden recipe.
DESCRIPTION: A painterly bench with two porcelain plaques (the target and your mix) and three glass jars whose fills rise as you pour. Each pigment carries a per-channel absorption matrix — cyan eats red, magenta eats green, yellow eats blue — and the mixed colour is the honest product of all three filters over white, so full pours of all three truly make mud-black. A percentage judge grades every MIX IT! and goads you accordingly; ≥97% streaks, reveals a reverse-searched recipe and fades in a new target.
TECHNIQUES: subtractive colour mixing via multiplicative per-channel absorption, random-but-plausible target generation from the same model, coarse-grid inverse search for recipe reveal, DOM jar-filling animation bound to range inputs, percentage colour-distance scoring.
INTERACTION: Pour cyan, magenta and yellow to match the left plaque, MIX IT! for a verdict, NEW TARGET if you surrender — and keep the streak alive to prove your eye.
