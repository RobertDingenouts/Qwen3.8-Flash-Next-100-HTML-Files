TITLE: Lunar Wheel — one synodic month, hand-drawn
PROMPT: Give the moon-phase widget real astronomy and real paint: compute the moon’s age from the 2000 new-moon epoch, draw the terminator as an honest ellipse, sprinkle hand-placed craters with rim light, and wrap the whole cycle in a ring you can spin.
DESCRIPTION: A night-sky card with a breathing starfield and a soft halo keyed to illumination. The moon renders procedurally — dark earthshine disc, then the clipped lit region built from a semicircle plus a terminator ellipse whose half-width is |cos θ|·R, waxing mirrored to waning — over a marbled maria wash and 38 seeded craters with highlight crescents. Below, the wheel of the month: 29 tick-marked days, a ray to today, and a tiny phase-correct moon orbiting the rim. A time-travel slider slides ±29 nights with a human-readable date readout.
TECHNIQUES: synodic-month arithmetic from epoch, terminator region via arc+ellipse clipping and mirroring, seeded crater/mare texture painting with rim-lighting, orbit-ring calendar with animated tick highlighting, illumination-keyed glow, date formatting via Intl.
INTERACTION: Drag the slider to travel a month through the phases (readout, name and wheel ray all follow), BACK TO TONIGHT snaps to the present, resize and the moon repaints itself.
