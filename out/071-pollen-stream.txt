TITLE: Pollen Stream — a meadow year, stacked in air
PROMPT: Draw the invisible weather of allergies as an airy streamgraph: five species blooming in gaussian waves across the whole year, stacked into one breathing ribbon, with a probe that reads the air at any day you point at.
DESCRIPTION: A soft meadow-palette streamgraph on warm paper: birch, grass, ragweed, olive and cypress each get a gaussian bloom keyed to their real-feeling peak day and width, multiplied by seeded daily noise so the ribbon has weather in it. Layers stack into a symmetric-ish flowing band over a month-labelled baseline. Hovering drops a dashed probe and floats a cream tooltip listing the day, each active species in its own colour with grain counts, and the total per cubic metre. Clicking a legend chip mutes that species and the stream re-flows live around the hole.
TECHNIQUES: gaussian seasonal kernels with seeded per-day jitter, layered streamgraph area rendering (upper/lower bound paths, reverse traversal close), mutable stacking that recomputes baselines on legend toggle, hover probe with nearest-day lookup and HTML tooltip composition, month tick placement on a day index axis.
INTERACTION: Sweep the pointer across the year to read the air, follow grass season swallow the chart in July, and click species chips to mute them — the meadow reorganises itself politely.
