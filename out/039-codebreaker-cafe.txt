TITLE: Codebreaker Café — four sugared tokens, two ways to play
PROMPT: Run Mastermind as a café service counter where you may be either the guest or the barista: six dessert-token colours, cherry/cream feedback pegs, and a barista AI that plays with a proper information-maximising strategy — so hiding a code is a duel, not a form.
DESCRIPTION: Two modes in one espresso-brown interface. YOU CRACK: the café picks a four-token code; you tap colours into slots and SERVE, reading cherries (right token, right place) and creams (right token, wrong place) down the column of ten rows. YOU HIDE: you set your own code, lock it, and the barista AI samples the full candidate set each turn, choosing the guess that minimises the worst-case remaining answers, while you honestly report cherries/creams with stepper buttons — it usually closes in around six.
TECHNIQUES: two-pass Mastermind scorer with duplicate handling, minimax expected-partition guessing from a shrinking consistent-candidate set, candidate filtering on reported feedback, DOM slot/peg composition with per-row feedback columns, mode state machine (set/lock/guess/report).
INTERACTION: Pick a colour in the tray, tap the four slots to place or clear tokens, SERVE to lock in a guess — or switch to YOU HIDE, build your secret, LOCK CODE, then answer each barista guess with the cherries/creams steppers and REPORT ▸.
