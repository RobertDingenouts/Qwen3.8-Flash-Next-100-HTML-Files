TITLE: Coral Growth — paint a living reef with reaction-diffusion
PROMPT: Let the visitor fertilise a dark sea-tank with a chemistry brush: two virtual chemicals diffuse and react into coral, mitosis, worms or maze stone, and every preset is a different climate they can still hand-tune with feed and kill dials.
DESCRIPTION: A full-screen Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion sim running on a coarse Float32 grid, upscaled softly onto the canvas. Chemicals A and B laplacian-diffuse at different rates while B autocatalyses where A is plentiful; a colour ramp turns the invisible chemistry into deep teal coral glowing to ivory tips. Dragging injects activator; five presets and live F/K sliders retune the physics instantly.
TECHNIQUES: dual-buffered Gray-Scott update with 3×3 diffusion kernel, offscreen low-res ImageData rendering upscaled via drawImage with DPR sizing, 256-entry colour LUT, pointer-coordinate remapping into grid space, floating instrument-panel UI with geometric sans type.
INTERACTION: Drag across the water to seed coral growth, tune feed F and kill K while it blooms, tap preset climates (coral, mitosis, worms, maze, diamond), and BLEACH REEF to sterilise the tank for new seeding.
