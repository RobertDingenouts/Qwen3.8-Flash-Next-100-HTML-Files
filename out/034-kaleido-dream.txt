TITLE: Kaleido Dream — cut a new stone and turn the drum
PROMPT: Build a parlour kaleidoscope with real glass behaviour: the instrument should be built from procedurally cut "stones" of stained gemlight, mirrored into perfect faceted wedges that the visitor turns, slides and zooms by hand.
DESCRIPTION: A full-screen optical drum: a randomly cut gem texture (layered radial glows in three random hues, luminous bezier veins, mineral speckle) is sampled through 4–16 mirrored wedges — alternating wedges flipped — around the screen centre. Drag horizontally to spin the drum, vertically to slide the glass through the aperture; a depth slider and mouse wheel zoom into the stone; CUT A NEW STONE shatters the current one and grows another.
TECHNIQUES: procedural stained-glass texture generation (hue triads, radial gradient blooms, bezier veins, noise speckle), wedge-by-wedge clip-path kaleidoscope compositing with alternating mirror transforms, texture pan via modulo wrapping, central vignette and facet rule overlays, DPR full-screen canvas.
INTERACTION: Drag to turn and slide the dream, wheel or DEPTH slider to zoom into the glass, FACETS slider re-cuts the drum from 4 to 16 sides, SPIN toggles the slow motor, and CUT A NEW STONE replaces the gem entirely.
