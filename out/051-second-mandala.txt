TITLE: Second Mandala — the day flowers at its own pace
PROMPT: A clock that gardens itself: every elapsed second must be permanently flowered onto the dial as one petal stroke, so by teatime the face is a watercolour mandala and midnight would be a full bloom.
DESCRIPTION: An analog watch face rendered over a persistent ink layer — at each new second a lens-shaped petal is stamped at that second’s angle with a hue drifting across the whole day, seeded by hour and minute. Gold second hand with a pulsing tick bulb, bone-white minute and hour hands, triple engraved rings with hour indexers, and an outer progress arc filling the day percentage with a live readout. The accumulated mandala survives every frame clear because it lives on its own double-resolution ink canvas.
TECHNIQUES: persistent offscreen ink layer composited under a live vector clock, polar petal drawing with quadratic lens curves, hue-cycling seeded by clock fields, day-fraction progress arc, DPR-scaled watch geometry, accelerated bloom mode that stamps 59 queued seconds.
INTERACTION: Let it bloom at real time — or press BLOOM A WHOLE MINUTE to fast-forward a minute of petals instantly, and FRESH DAWN to wipe the day’s flowers and start a clean dial.
