TITLE: Breath Pomodoro — twenty-five minutes, sixteen-second lungs
PROMPT: Bind the productivity timer to the autonomic nervous system: the pomodoro ring counts minutes, but the orb inside must be literally breathed with — four counts in, four held, four out, four held — all session long.
DESCRIPTION: A dark indigo studio with one instrument: a big progress ring tracking the 25:00 focus (or 5:00 break) countdown around a luminous breath orb pulsing on a free-running 4-4-4-4 box-breath cycle, shifting from cyan intake to amber release, with a live phase word and count under the ring. Finishing a focus block lights one of four coral tomato dots (remembered across visits), rings a two-note sine bell, and flips you into break. Tapping the orb itself also starts and stops the clock.
TECHNIQUES: independent breath oscillator phase-mapped to scale/colour/easing, dual-timer state machine (focus/break) with DOM countdown, localStorage habit dots, WebAudio bell with exponential decay envelopes, ring progress arc with rounded cap, DPR-scaled orb glow layering.
INTERACTION: Tap BEGIN (or the breathing orb) to start the block and follow the orb: in for four, hold, out for four, hold; SKIP jumps states, BELL rings the chime — four lit dots earn a longer break, doctor’s orders.
