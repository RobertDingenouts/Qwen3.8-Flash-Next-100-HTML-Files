TITLE: The Tarot of the Hour — same cards until the clock moves
PROMPT: A fortune that refuses to be spam: the spread is re-dealt only when the hour changes, drawn from eight cards rendered entirely in CSS geometry — conic-gradient suns, clip-path towers, inset-shadow crescents — over noir velvet and gold foil.
DESCRIPTION: A three-position spread (what passed / this hour / what turns up) seeded deterministically from the current UTC hour, so everyone reading at 3pm gets the same heaven and no one can refresh their way out of it. Each card is a 3D-flip div: the foil back carries a radial-gradient moon plate over engraved hatch lines; the face glows with its glyph — a rayed sun punched from a conic gradient with a radial mask, a crescent grown from inset box-shadow, a segmented wheel, an hourglass and key carved by clip-paths — under italic gold headings and a dry, kind reading. TURN flips all three; ARGUE WITH FATE shuffles the hour key if destiny feels like a suggestion.
TECHNIQUES: hour-seeded deterministic shuffling with periodic clock recheck, CSS 3D card flip with backface-visibility choreography, pure-CSS occult iconography (conic gradients, radial masks, clip-path polygons, inset-shadow crescents), gold-foil palette discipline.
INTERACTION: TURN THE CARDS to commit, click individual cards to peek and un-peek, and if the reading insults you, ARGUE WITH FATE — the spreadsheet remembers nothing, the hour forgives everything.
