TITLE: Pass Vault — forged locally, never leaves this tab
PROMPT: A bank-vault passphrase forge: turn the dial, watch the boltwork spin, and let crypto-grade randomness tumble out either as four-to-eight digestible words or as a hissing chaos string — with an honest entropy meter in bits.
DESCRIPTION: A brushed-steel vault door with a central hub that spins 300° on every forge; beneath it, the generated secret sits in a dashed copy-field and a strength bar colours itself red→amber→teal as entropy climbs toward 128 bits. PASSPHRASE mode draws words from a deduplicated ~110-word field lexicon and reports entropy as n·log₂(|lexicon|) — the diceware maths shown, not hidden; CHAOS STRING mode samples a 90-character alphabet via crypto.getRandomValues (with Math.random fallback) and reports the honest bits. Click the readout to copy.
TECHNIQUES: crypto.getRandomValues uniform sampling with graceful fallback, entropy-bit arithmetic displayed for both wordlist and charset modes, length-range semantics swapped per mode (words vs characters), vault-hub CSS rotation ceremony, clipboard copy with playful confirmation.
INTERACTION: PICK A MODE, slide LENGTH (3–8 words or 4–24 characters), hit FORGE ▸ to spin the bolts, and CLICK THE CODE to pocket it — everything is generated in your browser and forgotten on refresh.
